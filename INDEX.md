## Directory map

- `planning/`: current design plan.
- `research/decisions/`: selected conclusions and corrections, not every historical report.
- `research/scripts/`: one-off research/replay scripts preserved for provenance; they are not active benchmark entrypoints.
- `research/tests/`: tests belonging to those archived scripts.
- `evidence/`: manifests and evidence boundaries.

The archive intentionally omits raw model streams, credentials, game saves, generated caches and large `.bench-results` trees.
